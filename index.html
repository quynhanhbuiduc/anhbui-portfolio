🚶Human Pose Estimation:

  1. Context & Problem

Human pose estimation is a computer vision task that identifies human body keypoints from images or video and represents them as a 2D skeleton. The project explores how a lightweight deep learning model can be used to perform real-time multi-person pose estimation.

  1. Your Role

Contributed to understanding, implementing, and running the pose estimation pipeline using Python and PyTorch. Studied the model structure and the relationship between detected keypoints, heatmaps, and Part Affinity Fields (PAFs).

  1. Process & Artifacts

Worked with a PyTorch-based Lightweight OpenPose implementation to process images and video streams. The system detects body keypoints and connects them to generate 2D human skeletons. The project can be demonstrated through visual outputs from image/video or webcam inference.

Result
  1. Key Learnings

Gained practical experience with a computer vision and deep learning workflow, particularly model inference, data processing, and result visualization. The main challenge was understanding how individual body keypoints are associated to form complete human poses, which was addressed by studying the model pipeline and its visualization results.
